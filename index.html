Flap Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Flap Rush is a mobile arcade game developed and published by an independent developer.

Information We Collect

Flap Rush stores gameplay progress, high scores, achievements, and preferences locally on your device.

The game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to applicable privacy choices.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the game, save preferences and progress, display advertisements, measure ad performance, and maintain security.

Advertising

Flap Rush uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Ad personalization and consent options are provided where required.

Learn more about Google's data practices: Google Privacy and Terms.

Children and Teenagers

Flap Rush is not directed at children under 13. Advertising and privacy requirements for younger users are handled in accordance with applicable laws and platform policies.

Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device. Local data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Advertising data may be retained by Google according to its policies.

Data Sharing

Flap Rush does not operate its own account system or sell gameplay data. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners.

Your Privacy Choices

Where applicable, you can manage advertising privacy choices through the game's Settings screen or your device's privacy settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising services, or legal requirements change.
